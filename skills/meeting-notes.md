---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turn rough notes or a transcript into a summary, decisions and action items
---

Turn the meeting notes or transcript below into:

1. **Summary**: three to five sentences on what was discussed.
2. **Decisions**: a bulleted list of what was agreed.
3. **Action items**: a checklist, each with an owner and a due date if one was mentioned.
4. **Open questions**: anything left unresolved.

Keep the wording plain and leave out small talk. If something is unclear in the notes, say so instead of guessing.
